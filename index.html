TokenWallet

Add any ERC-20 token to your wallet in one click — with the correct symbol and decimals.

1. Connect your wallet

Works with any injected wallet: MetaMask, Trust Wallet (browser extension), TokenPocket, Coinbase Wallet, Exodus Web3, and more. This site never asks for your seed phrase or private key — it only asks your wallet to display a token.

3. Manual add — Ledger, Exodus & others

Ledger Nano S, Exodus and some wallets do not support the automatic prompt. Copy the details and add the token by hand:

  1. Open your wallet and go to Add custom token.
  2. Paste the contract address below, then the symbol and decimals.
  3. Confirm — the token appears with your real balance. Nothing leaves your device.
